Company Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home up to three days per week.

Employees must obtain approval from their immediate manager before working remotely.

Employees working from home must remain available during their normal working hours.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable and secure workspace.

Company confidential information must not be accessed from public computers.

Employees handling sensitive customer information must use the company VPN when working remotely.

Managers may require employees to work from the office when business requirements demand physical presence.
