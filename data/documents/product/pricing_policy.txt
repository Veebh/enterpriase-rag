Product Pricing Policy

The Enterprise Knowledge Assistant is offered in three plans.

The Starter plan supports up to 100 users.

The Business plan supports up to 1,000 users.

The Enterprise plan supports more than 1,000 users and includes advanced security controls.

Enterprise customers can configure custom document retention policies.

Enterprise customers can also integrate the assistant with corporate identity providers.

Pricing for the Enterprise plan is negotiated based on organizational requirements.
