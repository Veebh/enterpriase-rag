Enterprise Knowledge Assistant

The Enterprise Knowledge Assistant allows employees to ask natural language questions about company policies and product documentation.

The system uses retrieval augmented generation to retrieve relevant company documents before generating an answer.

The assistant provides source references with answers so users can verify the information.

The system supports documents from HR, Engineering, Security and Product departments.

The assistant should answer questions using the available company knowledge base.

If relevant information cannot be found in the knowledge base, the assistant should clearly state that the information is unavailable.
